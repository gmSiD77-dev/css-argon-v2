# LACMA Art+Technology Lab 2026: what the evaluators weigh
(Excerpt from the 2026 call for proposals. Full text in Greg's Claude project.)

Evaluation criteria:
1. Is the project artist-led and does it have artistic merit?
2. Does it explore emerging technology?
3. Does it suggest models, methods and/or data of interest to other artists and technologists?
4. Does the process include demos, prototypes or collaborative opportunities for the public during development?

Preference for: publicly accessible work; models, prototypes, data and code that can be shared broadly; public demonstration; "safe-to-fail" exploration over finished objects.
