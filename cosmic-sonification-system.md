## What this project is
The CSS is a large-scale immersive dome installation. Participants recline at the center of a 360-degree dome while the actual velocities, periods, and resonance ratios of the planets become sound, light, and geometry in real time.

This is not data visualization. It's cosmological historiography — the same celestial mathematics that ancient traditions independently encoded into spiritual architecture, made audible and felt in the body for the first time in a unified sensory experience.

**Central thesis:** Multiple wisdom traditions — separated by oceans and millennia — looked at the same sky and arrived at remarkably similar answers in different languages. The CSS makes that convergence experiential. You don't observe the cosmos. You hear it, feel it, and recognize it as yourself.

**Note on traditions (important):** The framework set is intentionally open and evolving. Rather than a fixed list of seven, the system is designed to grow — each new tradition introduced through a public session, ideally in partnership with the cultural community that carries that lineage. Current strong candidates: Kabbalistic, Vedic/Jyotish, Egyptian, Buddhist, Hermetic, Tarot, Mayan. The "traveling system" model — adding traditions as the installation tours new cities and regions — is an active creative and curatorial strategy, not a limitation.

**Philosophical spine:** *Musica universalis* — the doctrine traceable from Pythagoras through Kepler that planetary orbital ratios are the mathematical grammar of musical consonance. The cosmos doesn't just move. It plays. *As above, so below.*

## The creative model
Greg is the composer. The astronomical data and wisdom traditions function as conductor — shaping which music plays, which geometry emerges, which story unfolds. Every theme, timbre, and harmonic language originates from human artistic intention. The system performs from a composed score; it doesn't generate randomly.

### Layer 3: Compositional Architecture (Greg as composer)
The astrological chart layer determines "who" or "when":

- **Personal mode:** birth date/time/location → personalized cosmic blueprint
- **Historical mode:** any moment in history (pyramids at Giza, Buddha's enlightenment, the moon landing)
- **Real-time mode:** the current sky, live

The dominant planet (dignity-scored from the chart) becomes the lead voice. Sun sign sets harmonic center. Moon sign sets reverb/textural character (Earth Moon = dry/intimate; Water = long cathedral reverb; Fire = bright/short; Air = spacious with chorus). Planetary aspects determine consonance vs. dissonance between voices.

### The cymatics connection
Cymatics — the physical science demonstrating that sound produces geometric patterns (Chladni figures) — is the conceptual spine bridging scientific and sacred registers. The same frequency that generates the music produces the sacred geometry projected in the dome. The dome floor *could* function as a literal Chladni plate (concept only; vibroacoustic delivery is now leaning seating/wearables, Oct 2026). Sound *is* geometry. Scientifically defensible.

## Open architecture decisions
These are live questions — any collaborator should know nothing's locked:

1. **Root tuning system** — 432 Hz vs. 440 Hz vs. Pythagorean just intonation (leaning 432 Hz).
2. **Cosmic drone frequency** — Schumann resonance (7.83 Hz), Om/Earth year (136.1 Hz per Cousto), or dominant planet.
3. **Cousto frequencies: absolute pitch vs. ratios** — fixed Hz as literal notes, or Kepler intervals defining relationships with flexible root.
4. **Planet set** — 7 classical (POC) vs. expand to include Rahu/Ketu or outer planets in Phase 2.
5. **THE key architecture decision:** Do the traditions produce *different sounds* from the same chart, or *different narratives* from the same sound?
6. **Session duration** — 5, 10, or 20 minutes (or variable).
7. **Vibroacoustic layer frequency** — Schumann, Om/Earth 136.1 Hz, or 40 Hz gamma entrainment.
8. **Dome spatial mapping** — how 7 planets map to speaker positions across 12 house zones.
9. **Rhythmic character** — no pulse (purely textural), subtle planetary pulse, or evolutionary pulse.
10. **Software stack** — leaning Ableton + Max for Live + TouchDesigner; confirming with tech collaborator.
